About the Institution & Conference

What Is SATHAK 2026?

Mohamed Sathak AJ College of Engineering (MSAJCE), established in 2001 under the Mohamed Sathak Trust, is a premier autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University and approved by AICTE. Accredited with NAAC ‘A’ Grade (2023) and NBA (Mechanical – 2023), the institution is celebrating its Silver Jubilee (25 Years) of academic excellence. Spread across a 70-acre green campus at Siruseri, Chennai, MSAJCE fosters innovation, industry collaboration, and research-driven education.

SATHAK 2026 – International Conference on Sustainable and Advanced Technologies for Holistic Academic Knowledge is a multidisciplinary global platform hosted by MSAJCE to bring together academicians, researchers, and industry experts to share innovative research and sustainable technological advancements addressing industrial and societal needs.

Indexed Proceedings

All submitted manuscripts will undergo a strict double-blind peer review process conducted by experts from academia and industry to ensure the quality, originality, and technical relevance of the research work. Only papers that are accepted after review and presented at the conference will be eligible for publication.

Original research papers are invited from researchers, academicians, and industry professionals. All accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings with an ISBN number. Selected high-quality papers will be published in ICTACT Journals after a rigorous peer-review process.

Call for Contributions

Call for Papers & Submissions

SATHAK 2026 invites original, unpublished research papers from academicians, researchers, industry professionals, and scholars across the globe. The conference aims to provide a dynamic international platform to present and discuss emerging trends, innovative technologies, sustainable solutions, and transformative research across multidisciplinary domains.

📋 Submission Overview

It seeks to foster collaboration between academia and industry, encourage knowledge exchange, and promote research that addresses real-world industrial and societal challenges. High-quality theoretical, experimental, and applied research contributions are welcome, particularly those demonstrating innovation, practical, and interdisciplinary approaches.
